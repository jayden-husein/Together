# People enter Together only by their own consent

A Person exists in Together only if they signed up, or a venue shared a record they consented to. Family members, Coordinators and law enforcement cannot add a new Person or file a missing-person report; an Emergency Contact can only add a Can't-reach report to a traveler who named them. Anyone else is directed to contact law enforcement, whose own systems handle missing-person cases. We decided this so Together never becomes a database of people who didn't agree to be tracked, and so a stranger can't use a "report" to start following someone.

## Considered Options

- **Family members can report anyone**, as in the original plan. Rejected: it collects data on non-consenting people and duplicates law enforcement's job.
